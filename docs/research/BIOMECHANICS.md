# Biomechanics

## Purpose

Defines the biomechanical principles used throughout Vikzar AI.

---

# Overview

Biomechanics studies human movement.

The AI interprets movement using biomechanics rather than treating data as random numbers.

---

# Core Areas

- Running
- Sprinting
- Landing
- Cutting
- Jumping
- Deceleration
- Acceleration

---

# Important Concepts

Symmetry

Left vs Right movement quality.

---

Joint Loading

Mechanical stress placed on joints.

---

Ground Contact

Interaction between the foot and ground.

---

Impact

Landing forces.

---

Rotation

Angular movement of lower limbs.

---

Fatigue

Movement changes caused by muscular fatigue.

---

Movement Quality

Efficiency and stability of movement.

---

# Why Biomechanics Matters

Understanding biomechanics improves

- Injury prediction
- Fatigue estimation
- Movement analysis
- Performance evaluation

The AI should always interpret sensor data through biomechanical principles.
